Steering comes from the jet. No throttle means almost no steering (the ECU gives a little for 2.5 s). Lean into turns to carve. To slide a heavy ski: bars over, chop the throttle, then blip it back on, or stab the iBR and lean out. Trim up and full throttle makes the ski porpoise and leap; F pops it out of the water. Jump the wake boat's wake for big air. Tricks score points; chain them inside 3 s for a combo.

A jet ski simulator built on real Sea-Doo numbers: planing hull, jet pump steering, iBR, VTS trim and a rider whose weight actually moves the ski.
